THE NIGHTMARE BEGINS

Silence will not save you. The Silence: The Forest is an immersive extreme psychological horror survival experience built in Unity. You have been abandoned in the suffocating density of a pitch-black forest where every crackle is a death sentence.

The environment listens. The hostile entity lurking in the fog reacts directly to your sound emissions, your breath, and your movement speed. Your sole objective is to track down lost notes to piece together the tragic history of the woods before you are hunted down. Version v1.0 introduces the ruthless Hunter Mode, a completely purged interface, and a 3D positional audio system optimized for headphones.
