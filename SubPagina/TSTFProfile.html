SURVEILLANCE REPORT // SYNOPSIS

The Silence: The Forest is an immersive first-person psychological horror experience set deep within an unmapped, suffocating forest. An entity stalks your every movement relentlessly from the thick timberline shadows.

Your objective appears deceptively straightforward: navigate the dark expanse, retrieve 5 critical artifacts, and escape with your life. However, the anomaly dynamically adapts to your actions—visibility drops to absolute zero, and even the faintest noise triggers a lethal confrontation.

Mind the essential rule of this domain: silence is never safe.
